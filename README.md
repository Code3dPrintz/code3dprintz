Code-3dPrintz, an Arkansas small business that specializes in the creation and sale of custom 3D printed items. Our company prides itself on offering high-quality, made-to-order products that cater to various consumer needs, ranging from personalized items to professional-grade accessories.
In addition to our commitment to providing innovative products, Code-3dPrintz is dedicated to supporting the law enforcement community. A portion of the profits generated from our sales is donated to a non-profit organization dedicated to supporting law enforcement initiatives and promoting safety and community relations.
Our business is fully operational and eager to do our best to help those who are at their worst. We are committed to our mission of offering both value and positive impact to our customers and the communities we serve.
