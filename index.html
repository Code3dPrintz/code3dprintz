About Us

Hello! We are an Arkansas small business specializing in high-quality, made-to-order custom 3D printed items. We are committed to supporting law enforcement and providing our local and state agencies with funding for safer equipment, community events, or to give to their non-profit(s). So, you may be wondering what does "Code-3" mean in our business name? "Code-3" is a general First Responder Radio code for "Emergency Response". But why would we choose this name? After all, we are only nerds printing your imagination. We choose this name because far to often, our men and women behind the badge to not get recogniton or even a simple thank you for the sacrifices they make. We may not have the solution to every issue in the world, but we are choosing to give back and keep those who "Shake hands with Danger" safe. Thank you for taking the time to hear our story!
